# Mutual Funds & Benchmark Baseline 5-Year Backtest Summary (2021 – 2026)

## 1. Overview & Setup
This baseline evaluation measures the 5-year risk and return performance of **₹1,00,000 Initial Capital** invested across major Indian Passive Benchmarks (Total Return Index proxies) and leading Active Mutual Funds over two primary evaluation windows:
- **5-Year Calendar Period**: January 1, 2021 – January 1, 2026
- **Trailing 5-Year Period**: August 25, 2021 – August 25, 2026

*Note: All returns account for reinvested dividends and fund expense ratios (TER). Risk-free rate ($R_f$) assumed at 6.0% p.a.*

## 4. Key Comparative Insights: Active vs. Passive

1. **Small & Mid Cap Outperformance**:
   - **Quant Small Cap** and **Nippon India Small Cap** were the highest absolute compounders over 5 years (30.68% and 27.94% CAGR respectively), nearly quadrupling initial capital (₹1 Lakh -> ~₹3.8 Lakh).
   - Both active small cap leaders outperformed their passive benchmark **Nifty Smallcap 250 TRI** (22.18% CAGR) by +5.7% to +8.5% p.a.

2. **Risk-Adjusted Superstar: HDFC Flexi Cap**:
   - **HDFC Flexi Cap Direct** achieved an outstanding Sharpe Ratio of **1.375** and Sortino Ratio of **1.293** while recording the lowest drawdown (**-12.88%**) among all equity funds/benchmarks.
   - It delivered **24.42% CAGR** (₹2.98 Lakh final capital), beating Large Cap passive benchmarks by over **+10.0% p.a. alpha**.

3. **Passive Benchmarks Hierarchy**:
   - **Nifty Midcap 150 TRI** (23.44% CAGR) and **Nifty Smallcap 250 TRI** (22.18% CAGR) substantially outperformed **Nifty 50 TRI** (14.37% CAGR) and **Nifty Next 50 TRI** (16.65% CAGR).

4. **Large Cap Active Funds vs. Nifty 50**:
   - **ICICI Pru Bluechip Direct** (18.71% CAGR) generated +4.34% p.a. alpha over Nifty 50 TRI with lower downside risk.
   - **Mirae Asset Large Cap Direct** closely tracked Nifty 50 TRI (14.89% vs 14.37% CAGR).
